v1.0.0 — first public release

### Features
- **companies**: suppliers (also from the prop firm catalogue) and supplier groups, an item catalogue (also from the prop firms' plans), payments and receipts with taxes, other currencies and attached documents, stock at average cost, assets with depreciation, bank accounts with balances and a monthly payments summary
- **companies**: roles with the permissions they grant, members with or without an account, salaries, freelance receipts, contracts and documents, upcoming payments and company modules
- **finances**: earnings, expenses (with items, categories and subscriptions), debts and portfolios, each with a dashboard in EUR, USD or BRL converted at each day's rate, with hourly exchange rates
- **trading**: a strategy simulator on Binance and Coinbase candles, with a full report (summary, strategy, robustness proofs, entries and exits, hours), comparisons, other markets and live paper trading
- **calculators**: compound interest with saved simulations
- **admin**: users, external APIs, taxes, platforms (banks, brokers, exchanges and prop firms from PropFirmMap) and in-app updates, one version at a time
- **deploy**: one-line installer for a Proxmox VM (Debian 13, PostgreSQL, Garage, ClickHouse, nginx and the services)
